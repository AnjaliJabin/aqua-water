How It Works

From drain to pristine

A six-stage treatment journey that removes every contaminant — biological, chemical, and physical.

01

Screening & Grit Removal

Large solids, debris, and grit are mechanically removed before water enters the treatment chain.

02

Primary Sedimentation

Suspended solids settle under gravity in large clarifier tanks, reducing organic load by up to 60%.

03

Biological Aeration

Activated sludge process uses microorganisms to break down dissolved organic matter in aeration tanks.

04

Secondary Clarification

Biomass separates from treated water. Sludge is recycled back; clear effluent advances forward.

05

Tertiary Filtration

Sand filters and membrane systems remove residual suspended solids and fine particles.

06

UV Disinfection

Ultraviolet light eliminates 99.8% of pathogens — no chemicals, no residue, just clean water.
